your questions.

Not yet. Checkout isn’t open and no payment is taken on this site. You can build your box and reserve it with your email. We’ll email you first when boxes ship.

We save your email and the mix you built (pack size and how many of each flavor). It isn’t an order and there’s no payment or obligation. To change your mix, build it again and reserve with the same email; the newest version replaces the old one.

Waitlist (the email box at the bottom of most pages) saves your email for launch news.

Reserve your box saves your email plus your box mix, so we know what to make and can email you first.

Join rewards emails you a sign-in link that creates your normie account. Once checkout opens, you get 10% off your first box, each paid box earns a stamp, and 5 stamps get you a free box. Full rules are in our Terms.

There are no passwords. Enter your email and we send you a one-time sign-in link. Opening it signs you in on that device and, the first time, creates your account. The link expires after a short time; just request a new one if it does.

Prices for the 6-pack, 12-pack, and the normie special will be announced before launch and shown on the site per box and per stick. Reserving a box now doesn’t lock you into anything.

Pick a 6-pack or 12-pack of individually wrapped sticks and choose how many of each flavor go in. Or grab the normie special: 12 sticks, 4 of each flavor.

Tear open one stick, pour it into cold water or milk (8 oz for matcha and tiramisu coffee, 12 oz for açaí), and stir or shake for 15 seconds. Add ice. Matcha and tiramisu coffee also work hot.

Matcha and tiramisu coffee contain caffeine from the tea and coffee. Açaí is made without caffeine ingredients. The exact amount for each flavor prints on every box.

Açaí contains wheat and bovine collagen. It isn’t suitable for anyone with a wheat allergy or for a vegan diet.

Matcha and tiramisu coffee contain no major food allergens in the current formula. Each flavor page has its full Supplement Facts.
