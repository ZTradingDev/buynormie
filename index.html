how it works

tear. pour. stir.

  1. 1

    tear.

    Rip the top off one stick. Each one is a single serving.

  2. 2

    pour.

    Empty it into 8 to 12 oz of cold water or milk.

  3. 3

    stir.

    Stir or shake for 15 seconds. Add ice. That’s it.
