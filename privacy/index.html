what this covers

This policy explains what information normie (“normie,” “we,” “us”) collects through buynormie.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the website, our emails, and your normie account.

what we collect

Information you give us:

  • Your email address, when you join the waitlist, reserve a box, join rewards, or sign in.
  • Your box mix (pack size and how many of each flavor), when you reserve a box.
  • Your name, email, and message, when you use the contact form.
  • Once checkout opens: your order details, shipping address, and the rewards stamps tied to your email. Payment card details go directly to our payment processor; we never see or store them.

Information collected automatically:

  • Basic site events, such as a page view or a button click, with the page path and time. These are not linked to your name or email, and we don’t use cookies for them.
  • Our hosting and database providers keep standard server logs, including IP address and browser type, for security and reliability.

Information kept only on your device: your saved-flavor shortlist, your box draft, your motion setting, and, if you sign in, your sign-in session. These stay in your browser’s local storage.

We don’t use advertising cookies, third-party trackers, or social media pixels.

how we use it

  • To send you what you asked for: launch news, your reservation updates, sign-in links, your welcome discount, and rewards updates.
  • To plan production around the boxes people reserve.
  • To reply to your messages.
  • Once checkout opens: to process and ship orders, track rewards stamps, and send receipts.
  • To understand which pages and buttons people use, so we can improve the site.
  • To protect the site and our customers from abuse.

who we share it with

We never sell your information or share it for targeted advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run normie, under contracts that limit their use of it:

  • Supabase: our database, account sign-in, and sign-in emails.
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting.
  • Our email delivery provider, for launch and rewards emails.
  • Once checkout opens: our payment processor and shipping carriers.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of normie and our customers.

emails from us

When you give us your email through any form on this site, you agree to receive emails from normie about the launch, your reservation, your account, offers, and rewards. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. Order and account emails (like receipts and sign-in links) are sent as long as you have an account or an open order.

how long we keep it

We keep your email and reservation until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. We keep contact messages for up to 24 months. Once checkout opens, we keep order records as long as tax and accounting laws require. Site event data is kept for up to 24 months.

your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we have about you, correct it, delete it, or send you a copy. Email normieszn@gmail.com from the address you used with us. We respond within 45 days. We won’t treat you differently for making a request.

To stop marketing emails, use the unsubscribe link in any email or write to us. To clear what’s stored in your browser, use “clear my saved flavors” on the good to know page or clear your browser’s site data.

children

normie is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. Some normie flavors contain caffeine. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

security

Your data is protected by access controls, including database row-level security, and is sent over encrypted connections. Sign-in uses one-time email links, so there are no passwords to leak. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you if a breach affects it, as the law requires.

changes and contact

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top, and for significant changes we’ll email you. Questions? Write to normieszn@gmail.com.
